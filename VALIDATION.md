# Mac 0.4.11 verification

Unity 6000.3.7f1 universal Mono/Metal build for macOS 12 or later. The native executable and libraries contain Intel x86_64 and Apple Silicon arm64. 430/432 native assertions passed across macOS 15 Intel and Apple Silicon runners, with zero reported runtime issues: local updater install/restart/rollback fixtures, two-process session/result/rematch/lobby lifetime, ground recovery, stamina, scramble, standing stance, turning the corner, top control, hand contact, appearance/bone/texture/shoe checks, gym/rope geometry, and rendered startup/menu capture. The gym frame-rate benchmark failed on 2 virtual graphics runner(s); actual FPS and failures are preserved in raw reports. Physical-Mac performance remains unverified. Rendered menus and images do not establish smooth gameplay. Native screenshots were inspected separately. Reports are in release-evidence/0.4.11.

The app matches released Windows 0.4.11 gameplay/assets, with documented Mac platform adapters. Signing changes are confined to native signatures; managed code/assets remain byte-identical to the raw universal build. The source/build manifests identify raw and signed contents. ZIP permissions/CRC/hashes are checked; public downloads are verified after upload.

Same 92-body protocol 12 and Windows-hosted copied invites are retained. Current0.4.11 live internet play, human gameplay feel, long sessions, other macOS versions and physical keyboards/mice are not verified by these checks. This Mac version joins Windows-hosted invites; Mac hosting/controllers are not included. Native updater fixtures verified bad archive/URL/hash rejection, owned-app replacement/restart, failed-open rollback and stopping an unacknowledged owned child before rollback. Versions0.4.10 and earlier need one manual upgrade. Actual latest-public download/install/restart verification runs separately after publication.

Local ad-hoc signatures are verified on both native architectures. This is not Apple Developer ID signing or notarization; Gatekeeper may require Apple's per-app Open Anyway flow: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445 .

Current Windows gameplay limitations are documented in KNOWN_ISSUES.md. Native appearance assertions and screenshots do not certify all animation poses.
